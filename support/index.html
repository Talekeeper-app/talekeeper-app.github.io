Talekeeper Support

Talekeeper is a writing studio for long fiction in English and Thai, for iPhone and iPad (iOS 26 or later).

Contact: novelstudio.support@gmail.com. We read every message; replies may take a few days.

Getting started

In the Library, tap +, name your project and describe your story in one sentence. Everything else is optional, and you can start writing straight away.

Story Brief and Story Planner

The Story Brief holds your premise, genre, tone, length, chapters, point of view, words per chapter and writing style. The Story Planner shapes the whole novel (beginning, middle and end) into chapters and scenes. Plans never change what you have already written.

Novel Run

The Novel Run card at the top of a project always shows your next step: plan the novel, approve the plan, then draft chapter by chapter. You can do each step with your AI or write it yourself.

Use with your AI (Handoff)

  1. Tap Prepare AI Prompt, then Copy Prompt.
  2. Paste it into the AI you already use.
  3. Copy the AI's whole answer, tap Paste in Talekeeper, then Import Answer.
  4. Review what was found. Nothing is added to your story until you accept it.

No AI subscription is needed to write, plan or export. If iOS asks "Allow Paste?", that is iOS protecting your clipboard: Talekeeper reads it only when you tap Paste. If an answer is not recognized, paste the complete answer, including its first and last lines.

Story State

Story State (in the project menu) shows what Talekeeper knows about your story: who is where, who has what, and what each character, and the reader, knows. After a chapter, use Check Story State to review the changes. Nothing changes without your approval.

Writing Style

In the Story Brief, choose a Writing Style, such as Clear & Direct, Lyrical, Noir or Light Novel, and add your own style notes if you like. New chapter prompts follow it.

Chapters

Chapters are numbered automatically in reading order. Use the chapter's ⋯ button to rename it, add a scene, add a chapter after it, read it, or delete it. Deleted chapters and scenes go to Trash (project menu), where you can restore them.

Export

Project menu → Export: plain text (TXT), Markdown, PDF, a Talekeeper archive (.novelarchive) or an Open Archive (.zip) that other apps can read.

Backup and restore

  • Project menu → Backup & Recovery can save automatic backups to a folder you choose.
  • To move a project to another device, export a .novelarchive and open it on the new device.
  • Your device's iCloud or computer backup can also restore Talekeeper together with your projects.

Troubleshooting

  • Something looks wrong: close Talekeeper and open it again. If the problem continues, email us.
  • A project won't open: Talekeeper offers to recover it from its automatic snapshots.
  • Before deleting the app: export or back up your projects first, because deleting the app removes them from the device.
  • Language: choose English or Thai in Settings → Language.

Privacy

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your projects stay on your device. Read the Privacy Policy.
