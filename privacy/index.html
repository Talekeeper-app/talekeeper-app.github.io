Talekeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026
Contact: novelstudio.support@gmail.com

Summary

Talekeeper is a writing app for iPhone and iPad. Your projects are stored on your device.

  • There is no Talekeeper account and no Talekeeper server.
  • Talekeeper does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it does not collect identifiers.
  • Talekeeper sends information to another service only when you choose to use one: an AI service you set up, or a research search you start.

What is stored on your device

Talekeeper keeps your work in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. This includes:

  • your manuscripts, chapters and scenes;
  • story briefs and story plans;
  • Story State: the people, places and objects in your story, and what characters and readers know;
  • research notes and sources;
  • the AI answers you paste in or receive;
  • images you add;
  • your settings.

Device backups. Talekeeper does not use iCloud or CloudKit itself. Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer) may include Talekeeper's data, depending on your Apple backup settings.

Backups and exports you create. If you turn on Talekeeper's backup, it saves copies to a folder you choose. Exports (such as TXT, Markdown, PDF or archive files) are saved where you choose. If you choose a cloud folder, the file is stored there under that service's terms.

Using an AI you choose (copy and paste)

Talekeeper can prepare a prompt from your story. The prompt is created on your device, and Talekeeper does not send it anywhere. You decide whether to copy or share it, and where to paste it: for example, an AI app or website you already use. That service handles what you give it under its own privacy policy and terms. You then paste the answer back into Talekeeper, and nothing is added to your story until you review it.

Clipboard

Talekeeper reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste in the app. iOS may ask you to allow this.

Optional AI on your own computer

If you set up an AI server on your own computer (for example a Mac on your local network), Talekeeper sends the text needed for an AI task you start to that server only. Talekeeper never switches to a cloud AI service on its own.

Optional cloud AI with your own key

You can connect a cloud AI provider using your own account and API key. Talekeeper has built-in support for Anthropic (Claude) and Google Gemini, and it can also connect to another AI service that uses an OpenAI-compatible interface, including one running on your own computer, if you enter its address yourself. Nothing is connected until you connect it, and there is no default provider. If you do connect one:

  • Talekeeper sends that provider the content needed for an AI task you start: your instructions and the related parts of your story.
  • It asks for your permission before it first sends anything to a provider. It never sends story content from projects you mark Private or Offline, and it never falls back to a cloud provider automatically.
  • You can withdraw that permission at any time, one provider at a time, in Settings › AI › Privacy and AI activity: tap Revoke beside the provider. Talekeeper then sends that provider nothing until you allow it again. Removing the provider altogether also deletes its API key from your device's Keychain. Content a provider has already received is covered by that provider's own policy, so ask the provider to delete it there.
  • Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain and is sent only to that provider.
  • The provider processes the content under its own privacy policy and terms, and under your agreement with it. Retention, human review and use for training depend on the provider and on your plan with it. Please read the terms of the provider you choose.
  • Talekeeper adds no terms of its own to that relationship and takes no rights over your writing. It asks the provider only to answer the request you started, keeps no copy outside your device, and shares your content with no one else. Because the account and the key are yours, the protection your writing receives is the protection in that provider's own terms for the plan you are on — which is why your permission is asked before anything is sent, and why you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Free plans can differ. Paid API use generally excludes your content from training, while some providers' free or experimental tiers allow them to use what you submit to improve their services and models. Google states this difference for the Gemini API explicitly. If you do not want your manuscript used that way, use a paid API plan, mark the project Private or Offline, or write without cloud AI — every feature in Talekeeper works without it.

Research

Research features are optional and run only when you use them.

  • Quick Research sends the search terms you see in its preview to public research services: encyclopedia and data services, scholarly and library catalogues, a scholarly index if you add its key, or a search server you configure yourself. Invented names from your story and your manuscript text are not sent by default.
  • Archived copies. Looking up an archived copy of a page sends that page's address to the Internet Archive.
  • Adding a web page downloads the page at the address you give.
  • Search the Web opens your search in a browser view.

These services may keep ordinary server logs, such as your IP address and the request, under their own privacy policies.

Some links in Talekeeper open a web page: a research result or source you tap, and the help, support and policy links. Depending on the link, the page opens inside the app in a Safari view or in your own browser. Either way you are then on someone else's website, under their privacy policy, and that site can see your request as any website does. Talekeeper adds nothing of yours to those addresses.

Photos and files

Images you add are chosen with the system picker, so Talekeeper does not need access to your whole photo library. Images are stored with location and other private metadata removed. Imports read only the files you choose.

Purchases

The current version of Talekeeper has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Deleting your data

  • Inside a project: scenes, chapters and other items you delete go to the project's Trash, and emptying the Trash removes them permanently.
  • Whole projects: the current version does not have a separate "delete project" command. Deleting the Talekeeper app removes all its projects and settings from that device. Device backups may still contain them until those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • Your copies: exported files and backups you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them.
  • API keys: you can remove them in the app's AI Settings.
  • Content you sent to other services: anything you chose to send to an AI provider or a research service is handled under that service's policy. Talekeeper cannot delete it there.

Children

Talekeeper is a general writing tool without accounts, chat, publishing or social features. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If Talekeeper's data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated.
